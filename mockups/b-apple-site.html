Movement 01

Conventional Deadlift.

Hinge at the hips. Drive the floor away. The foundation of a strong back.
4×5Sets × Reps
3:00Rest
RPE 8Effort
  • Setup. Bar over mid-foot. Shins close. Neutral spine.
  • Drive. Push the floor down. Don't pull with your back.
  • Lockout. Hips and knees finish together. No overextension.
At Work Gym: dumbbell RDL (3×8) if no bar available.
Movement 02

Weighted Pull-Up.

Full stretch at the bottom. Chin clears the bar. Slow the way down.
3×8Sets × Reps
2:00Rest
SlowEccentric
  • Grip. Shoulder-width, palms forward. Thumbs around.
  • Pull. Think elbows into back pockets. Chest to bar.
  • Lower. Three seconds down. Full arm extension.
At Work Gym: lat pulldown (3×10) matched for effort.
Movement 03

Chest-Supported Row.

Chest pinned. Elbows drive back. Squeeze one second at the top.
4×10Sets × Reps
1:30Rest
1s holdTop
  • Chest. Pressed into the pad. Don't lift off.
  • Elbows. Drive down and back, not flared wide.
  • Squeeze. One-second pause, then control the return.
At Work Gym: bench-supported dumbbell row, same rep scheme.
Movement 04

Barbell Curl.

Elbows locked at your sides. Forearms do the work. No swing.
4×8Sets × Reps
1:30Rest
Full ROMRange
  • Stance. Shoulder-width. Core tight. Ribs down.
  • Lift. Curl without swinging. Elbows pinned.
  • Lower. Two seconds. Full extension at the bottom.
At Work Gym: same exercise — dumbbell or EZ-bar both work.
