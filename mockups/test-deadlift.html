Conventional Deadlift.

Every joint is articulated. The bar stays in the hands. If the figure does it right, so do you.

    What you should see

  • 1. Feet planted, bar over mid-foot.
  • 2. Hips go back before the bar leaves the floor. Knees barely move.
  • 3. Back stays straight the whole time — torso rotates as one piece around the hip.
  • 4. Arms hang straight down like ropes. Bar tracks the shin, then the thigh.
  • 5. Lockout: hips and knees finish together. No leaning back.
