Push.

Chest, triceps, and front delts. Press heavy early, chase volume late.

Warm-up · 6 min

Mobilise shoulders. Prep the press.

Rowing machine. Easy pace.3 min
Long, smooth strokes. Legs drive first, then hips, then arms. Inhale on the recovery.
Band pull-aparts. Chest high, pinch blades together.2 × 15
Arms straight, no shrug. Pull the band apart until it touches the chest. Slow return.
Scapular push-ups. Hands on the bar or floor, protract/retract.2 × 10
Push-up position with locked arms. Only the shoulder blades move — protract (up) and retract (down). No elbow bend.
Arm circles. Controlled, small then large.10 each direction
Small controlled circles first. Build to large circles. Both directions. Keep the ribs down.
Bench ramp sets. Empty bar → 40% → 60% → 80% of working weight.8/5/3/3
Ramp up to your first working set. Each set gets heavier but you do fewer reps — no failure, no close-to-failure. The last ramp set should feel crisp, not hard.
Movement 01

Barbell Bench Press.

Feet planted, back arched, bar to the lower chest. The king of upper-body size.
4×6-8Sets × Reps
3:00Rest
RPE 8Effort
  • 1. Shoulder blades pinched and pressed into the pad. Small arch in the low back.
  • 2. Lower to the nipple line. Elbows ~75° from the torso, not flared to 90°.
  • 3. Drive feet through the floor as you press. Bar travels in a slight J-curve.
Swap. Work Gym: flat DB bench press, 4×8 (safer to solo-fail). Lifetime: same lift, use a spotter on the last set.
Movement 02

Incline Dumbbell Press.

Bench set to 30°. Dumbbells press up and slightly together. Hits the upper chest.
3×8-10Sets × Reps
2:00Rest
30°Incline
  • 1. Set the bench to 30°. Shoulder blades pinned into the pad.
  • 2. Lower until the dumbbells graze the upper chest. Don't flare — ~45° from ribs.
  • 3. Press up and slightly together. Stop short of locking out to keep tension on the chest.
Swap. Work Gym: same lift. Lifetime: landmine press for variation or incline Smith machine for safer failure.
Movement 03

Weighted Dips.

Forward lean for chest. Upright for triceps. Either way — full range, no bouncing.
3×8-10Sets × Reps
2:00Rest
FullRange
  • 1. Grip the bars, lock out, knees bent or crossed for balance.
  • 2. Lean forward for chest emphasis — upper arms parallel to the floor at the bottom.
  • 3. Press to a near-lockout. Don't slam out — stop just short.
Swap. If under 8 clean bodyweight reps: assisted dip machine 3×10. Work Gym: bench dips (feet elevated) as a fallback.
Movement 04

Cable Fly — High to Low.

Pulleys high, arms sweep down and together. Squeeze hard for one full second in front.
3×12-15Sets × Reps
60sRest
1sSqueeze
  • 1. Pulleys at the top. Step forward to the mid-point between them.
  • 2. Slight elbow bend, locked throughout. Sweep down and across to meet below the chest.
  • 3. Squeeze one second in front. Control the stretch on the way back.
Swap. Work Gym: DB fly on flat or incline bench. Lifetime: pec deck machine for a clean finisher.
Movement 05

Overhead Tricep Extension.

Upper arms pinned next to your ears. Only the forearms move. Full stretch at the bottom.
3×10-12Sets × Reps
90sRest
DeepStretch
  • 1. Stand or seat against a back pad. Elbows tight to the head.
  • 2. Lower slow behind the head. Feel a deep stretch in the long head.
  • 3. Drive the weight up until the elbows lock. Don't flare.
Swap. Work Gym: single DB overhead (both hands under the bell). Lifetime: rope overhead cable extension — constant tension.
Movement 06

Rope Pushdown.

Elbows glued to your ribs. Press the rope down and spread at the bottom.
3×12-15Sets × Reps
60sRest
SpreadAt Bottom
  • 1. Stand tall, slight forward lean. Elbows glued to the ribcage.
  • 2. Only the forearm moves. If the elbow drifts, drop the weight.
  • 3. At the bottom, spread the rope apart and hold one second.
Swap. Work Gym: single-arm band pushdown if no cable. Lifetime: V-bar or straight bar for variety each week.

Shoulder cross-body stretch. Arm across chest, pull with the other.30s each

Pull.

Back and biceps. Lats, mid-back, rear delts, and arms — in that order.

Warm-up · 6 min

Prime the chain.

Rowing machine. Easy pace.3 min
Long, smooth strokes. Legs drive first, then the hips, then the arms. Inhale on the recovery.
Band pull-aparts. Chest high, pinch shoulder blades.2 × 15
Arms straight, no shrug. Pull the band apart until it touches the chest. Slow return.
Scapular pull-ups. Hang from the bar; shrug down and back.2 × 5
Arms stay straight. Only the shoulder blades move — pack them down and back. You should rise an inch or two, no more.
Cat–camel. Slow. Thoracic mobility.2 × 8
On hands and knees. Round the spine toward the ceiling (cat), then arch and look up (camel). Move with your breath.
Dead-hangs. Hang from the pull-up bar, shoulders packed down.2 × 20s
Grip the bar, hang dead — arms straight. Then pack the shoulders away from the ears. Stretches the lats and decompresses the spine.
Movement 01

Weighted Pull-Up.

Full hang at the bottom, chin clears the bar at the top. Control the descent.
3×6-8Sets × Reps
2:00Rest
3sEccentric
  • 1. Shoulder-width grip, palms forward. Thumbs around the bar.
  • 2. Drive elbows down and back. Think "chest to bar," not "chin to bar."
  • 3. Lower under control — three full seconds to dead hang.
Swap. If under 6 clean reps: lat pulldown 3×8-10 with the same effort level. Add weight (belt or held DB) only when you hit 8 clean reps unweighted.
Movement 02

Barbell Row.

Stay bent over. Drive the elbows up and back. Squeeze the shoulder blades together.
4×8Sets × Reps
2:00Rest
1sSqueeze
  • 1. Hinge to ~45°. Flat back, chest proud, core braced.
  • 2. Pull to the lower ribs, not the chest. Elbows track past the body.
  • 3. One-second squeeze at the top. Control all the way down.
Swap. Work Gym: chest-supported DB row (kills the cheating). Lifetime: T-bar row or Meadows row.
Movement 03

Seated Cable Row.

Long pull, full stretch at the front. Pull the elbows past your sides.
3×10-12Sets × Reps
1:30Rest
2sStretch
  • 1. Chest tall. Feet pressed into the plate. No rounded lower back.
  • 2. Let the shoulder blades protract at the front — feel the full stretch.
  • 3. Pull the handle to the lower belly. Elbows brush past your sides.
Swap. Work Gym: one-arm DB row or seated machine row. Lifetime: V-handle or wide-grip variation — rotate each week.
Movement 04

Face Pull.

Elbows flare wide and high. Rope splits at the face. Shoulder health in a single exercise.
3×15Sets × Reps
60sRest
LightLoad
  • 1. Rope at eye level. Palms face each other, thumbs back.
  • 2. Pull the rope apart as you pull toward your face. Elbows high and wide.
  • 3. Pause one second with hands beside your ears. Shoulder blades pinned.
Swap. Work Gym: band face pulls (anchor to the squat rack). Lifetime: reverse pec-deck as an alternative.
Movement 05

Barbell Curl.

Elbows pinned to your sides. The forearms do everything. No swing.
4×8Sets × Reps
1:30Rest
2sDown
  • 1. Stand tall. Core braced. Elbows glued to the ribcage.
  • 2. Curl without moving the elbow. If it drifts forward, lighten the load.
  • 3. Two seconds down. Straighten the arm fully at the bottom.
Swap. EZ-bar curl works the same (kinder on wrists). Avoid standing DB curls as the anchor here — elbows cheat too easily.

Lat stretch. Grab a rack, hinge, sit back.45s each side
Grip the rack with one hand. Hinge at the hips and sit your butt back away from the bar. Feel the lat stretch from armpit down the side.
Biceps stretch. Arm against a wall, rotate away.30s each
Palm flat on a wall, fingers pointing back. Slowly rotate your body away from the wall to lengthen the bicep.
Child's pose. Breathe into the lower back.60s
Knees wide, big toes touching. Sit back onto the heels and reach the arms long in front. Breathe into the lower back.

Legs.

Squat, hinge, and everything they drive. Quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves, core.

Warm-up · 8 min

Open the hips. Wake the quads.

Bike or rower. Moderate pace, build a light sweat.4 min
Moderate pace. You should be able to hold a conversation but not comfortably.
Bodyweight squats. Deep, slow, full range.2 × 10
Deep — hips below parallel. Chest tall. Think two seconds down, drive up.
Leg swings. Front-back then side-to-side.10 each / leg
Hold a rack or wall. Swing the working leg fully forward and back — no twisting the hips. Then repeat side-to-side.
90/90 hip rock. Front leg 90°, back leg 90°, sit tall, switch.30s each side
Sit on the floor. Front leg bent 90° in front, back leg bent 90° beside. Rock from side to side with a tall spine.
Squat ramp sets. Empty bar → 40% → 60% → 80% of working weight.8/5/3/3
Ramp into your first working set. Each set gets heavier and has fewer reps. Last ramp set should move crisp, not hard — save the grinding for working sets.
Movement 01

Back Squat.

Hips go back and down together. Knees track over toes. Chest stays proud all the way down.
4×6-8Sets × Reps
3:00Rest
RPE 8Effort
  • 1. Bar on the traps (high-bar) or rear delts (low-bar). Wrists stacked.
  • 2. Brace hard. Break at hips and knees together. Knees out.
  • 3. Hips at or below knee crease at the bottom. Drive the floor away to stand.
Swap. Work Gym: goblet squat with heaviest DB, 4×10. Lifetime: Smith squat or hack squat machine for bad-back days.
Movement 02

Conventional Deadlift.

Hinge at the hips. Drive the floor away. The single biggest strength-to-size return in the gym.
3×5Sets × Reps
3:00Rest
RPE 8Effort
  • 1. Bar over mid-foot. Shins close. Neutral spine, chest up.
  • 2. Hips go back before knees. Push the floor away.
  • 3. Lockout: hips and knees finish together. No overextension.
Swap. Work Gym: trap-bar deadlift (back-friendly) or dumbbell RDL. Lifetime: straps OK after the first heavy set.
Movement 03

Standing Calf Raise.

Toes on a plate. Full stretch at the bottom, full contraction at the top.
4×12-15Sets × Reps
60sRest
2sStretch
  • 1. Balls of feet on the plate. Heels hang free with room to drop.
  • 2. Drive the balls of the feet into the plate — not the toes.
  • 3. Pause one second up and two seconds down. Don't bounce.
Swap. Work Gym: DB calf raise (one DB, opposite hand). Lifetime: standing calf raise machine or smith machine.
Movement 04

Hanging Leg Raise.

Straight legs, if you can. No swing. Curl the pelvis under at the top.
3×10-12Sets × Reps
60sRest
No swingStrict
  • 1. Shoulder blades packed down. Dead hang, no swing.
  • 2. Raise legs with a controlled, slow pelvic curl — belly does the work.
  • 3. Above horizontal if you can. Lower slowly — no dropping.
Swap. Work Gym: captain's-chair leg raise (no hang needed). Lifetime: same — or weighted decline sit-up as an alternative.
Movement 05

Bulgarian Split Squat.

Rear foot up. Front leg does everything. Brutal on quads and glutes with moderate load.
3×10 / legSets × Reps
2:00Rest
UnilateralPattern
  • 1. Rear foot on the bench (laces or toes). Front foot far enough forward that the shin stays near vertical.
  • 2. Drop straight down — the knee stays over the foot. Chest tall.
  • 3. Drive through the front heel to stand. Finish all reps on one side, then switch.
Swap. Work Gym: DBs in each hand — same reps. Lifetime: Smith-machine split squat if balance is an issue.
Movement 06

Lying Leg Curl.

Hips pressed into the pad. Heels to glutes. Slow on the way down.
3×12Sets × Reps
60sRest
3sDown
  • 1. Knees just past the edge of the pad. Pelvis pressed flat.
  • 2. Point the toes, curl the heels to the glutes. Don't lift the hips off the pad.
  • 3. Three seconds on the lower. Squeeze the hamstrings at the top.
Swap. Work Gym: single-leg DB Romanian deadlift, 3×10/leg (hamstring dominant). Lifetime: seated leg curl as an alternative.

Delts.

Shoulders, traps, and core. Width, cap, and a bulletproof trunk.

Warm-up · 6 min

Open the shoulders. Press nothing cold.

Rowing machine. Easy pace.3 min
Long, smooth strokes. Legs drive first, then hips, then arms. Easy breathing.
Band dislocates. Wide grip, slow, full rotation.2 × 10
Hold a band wide (shoulders-width + 10"). Bring it overhead, behind the back, and return. Arms straight throughout.
Wall slides. Back flat, elbows glue to the wall.2 × 8
Back, hips, and head flat to the wall. Slide the forearms up like a goal-post — elbows and wrists stay in contact.
Scapular push-ups. Protract and retract, no elbow bend.2 × 10
Push-up position with locked arms. Only the shoulder blades move. Shoulders come away from the ears at the bottom.
Press ramp sets. Empty bar → 40% → 60% → 80%.8/5/3/3
Ramp into the first working set. Each set heavier, fewer reps. Press each ramp set with working-weight form — strict, no leg drive.
Movement 01

Overhead Press.

Bar at the clavicle, straight up. Head through the window at lockout. Glutes tight.
4×6-8Sets × Reps
2:30Rest
RPE 8Effort
  • 1. Bar on the clavicle. Elbows just under and in front of the bar.
  • 2. Squeeze glutes, brace core. Don't let the ribs flare.
  • 3. Press up, then pull the head through at lockout. Bar over the mid-foot.
Swap. Work Gym: seated DB shoulder press against a bench. Lifetime: landmine press for shoulder-friendly days.
Movement 02

Cable Lateral Raise.

Lead with the elbow. Stop at shoulder height. Slow all the way down.
4×12-15Sets × Reps
60sRest
3sDown
  • 1. Cable low, slight lean toward the working side. Hand starts across the body.
  • 2. Raise with the elbow — wrist and hand stay below it the whole way.
  • 3. Peak at shoulder height, not above. Three seconds to lower.
Swap. Work Gym: DB lateral raise — same reps, slightly lighter. Both gyms: use cable when possible (constant tension).
Movement 03

Rear Delt Fly.

Bent over at the hips. Arms sweep out to the sides, pinky up at the top.
3×12-15Sets × Reps
60sRest
SlowTempo
  • 1. Bent over ~45°. Chest up, flat back, soft knees.
  • 2. Lead with the pinky. Sweep wide — don't row.
  • 3. Peak when arms are parallel to the floor. Squeeze, then lower.
Swap. Work Gym: chest-supported on an incline bench (eliminates low-back fatigue). Lifetime: reverse pec-deck — constant tension.
Movement 04

Dumbbell Shrug.

Shoulders straight up to the ears. Hold one second at the top. No rolling.
3×12Sets × Reps
60sRest
1sSqueeze
  • 1. Heavy dumbbells at your sides. Chin tucked, chest proud.
  • 2. Elevate the shoulders straight up — don't roll them.
  • 3. Hold one second at the top. Lower with control.
Swap. Work Gym: barbell shrug in front of the body. Lifetime: trap-bar shrug for the heaviest loads.
Movement 05

Weighted Plank.

Forearms down, body flat as a board. Glutes squeezed. Plate on the back if it's easy.
3×45sSets × Hold
60sRest
TightGlutes
  • 1. Forearms under shoulders. Hands relaxed, not clenched.
  • 2. Glutes squeezed hard, tuck the pelvis — no sagging hips or raised butt.
  • 3. Breathe shallow and steady. Add a plate when you can hold 60s clean.
Swap. Work Gym: cable crunch (kneeling, rope at the back of the neck) — 3×15 for dynamic core work. Lifetime: ab-wheel rollouts for advanced.

Arms.

Biceps, triceps, forearms. Low-fatigue day that still adds real size.

Warm-up · 5 min

Mobilise elbows and wrists.

Rowing machine. Easy pace.3 min
Long, smooth strokes. Legs drive first, then hips, then arms.
Band pull-aparts. Chest high.2 × 15
Arms straight, no shrug. Pull the band until it touches the chest. Slow return.
Wrist and elbow circles. Big, slow.30s each
Big circles with the fists — wrists first, then elbows. Both directions, both joints.
Ramp-up set. Empty bar or light weight on the first lift.1 × 12
One light, easy set on the day's first movement. No strain — this is nervous-system priming, not fatigue.
Movement 01

Close-Grip Bench Press.

Shoulder-width grip. Elbows tucked tight. The triceps take over from the chest.
4×8Sets × Reps
2:00Rest
TightElbows
  • 1. Grip just inside the shoulders. Wrists stacked over the elbows.
  • 2. Elbows stay tucked to your ribs the entire way down.
  • 3. Bar lowers to the lower sternum, then press and lock out.
Swap. Work Gym: floor press (same grip) — saves the shoulders on heavy days. Lifetime: board press or Swiss bar if available.
Movement 02

Skullcrusher.

Upper arms vertical. Bar comes down behind the head, not to the forehead. Long-head stretch.
3×10Sets × Reps
90sRest
DeepStretch
  • 1. Lie flat. Upper arms perfectly vertical — don't let them drift toward the face.
  • 2. Lower the bar behind the head (to the bench), not to the forehead.
  • 3. Press up until elbows lock. Full tricep contraction at the top.
Swap. Work Gym: DB "JM press" variation — easier on elbows. Lifetime: EZ-bar if wrists complain, or use cables for constant tension.
Movement 03

EZ-Bar Curl.

Bent bar — kinder on the wrists. Elbows pinned, strict curls.
4×8-10Sets × Reps
1:30Rest
StrictForm
  • 1. Shoulder-width grip on the narrow bend. Elbows tight to the ribs.
  • 2. Curl without swinging. If the elbows shift forward, go lighter.
  • 3. Full extension at the bottom, full contraction at the top.
Swap. Work Gym: standing DB curl with strict form. Lifetime: preacher curl machine for extra elbow stability.
Movement 04

Preacher Curl.

Upper arms pinned to the pad. The short head of the biceps can't hide — strict, deep curl.
3×10-12Sets × Reps
60sRest
FullStretch
  • 1. Chest and armpits against the pad. Upper arms locked to the angle.
  • 2. Fully extend at the bottom — don't cheat the stretch. Elbows stay on the pad.
  • 3. Curl until the forearm is vertical. Squeeze, then lower for a count of three.
Swap. Work Gym: single-arm DB preacher over an incline bench. Lifetime: preacher machine with cable or plate-loaded arm.
Movement 05

Hammer Curl.

Neutral grip — thumbs up. Builds the brachialis and the brachioradialis (size under the biceps, and forearms).
3×12Sets × Reps
60sRest
StrictForm
  • 1. Dumbbells at your sides, thumbs forward. Palms face each other.
  • 2. Curl with zero elbow drift. Squeeze at the top.
  • 3. Lower slowly. Full arm extension at the bottom of every rep.
Swap. Work Gym: same lift — dumbbells at both gyms. Lifetime: cross-body hammer curl for brachialis emphasis.
Movement 06

Reverse Curl.

Palms down. Hits the brachioradialis and forearms. Lighter than a regular curl — expect it.
3×12-15Sets × Reps
45sRest
StrictForm
  • 1. Overhand grip — knuckles up. EZ-bar or straight bar.
  • 2. Curl without swinging. The forearm does more work here than the biceps.
  • 3. Finish with a squeeze at the top, then slow on the way down.
Swap. Work Gym: DB reverse curl. Lifetime: cable rope reverse curl for constant tension.
