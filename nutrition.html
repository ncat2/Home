Supplements

Three. That's it.

More isn't better. These three have actual evidence and fit your goals (size + heart).
Strength + Size

Creatine Monohydrate

1st Phorm
Dose5 g daily
TimingAnytime — consistency > timing
WithWater (lots) and any meal
Why. Strength, size, and brain. Saturates over ~3 weeks; effects compound. The most studied legal supplement in lifting. Note: 5 mg would be 1/1000th of a dose — confirm the label says 5 g (or 5,000 mg) per scoop.
Recovery + Heart

Magnesium Glycinate

1st Phorm
Dose200-400 mg
TimingEvening (helps sleep)
WithA small meal — empty stomach can upset
Why. Muscle relaxation, sleep quality, electrolyte balance. Mild evidence for reducing arrhythmia risk, especially when low magnesium is the trigger. Glycinate is the gentle, well-absorbed form. Heads up: recurrent arrhythmia after alcohol is "holiday heart" — magnesium helps but a cardiologist should confirm it's benign.
Protein

Whey Protein

Dymatize ISO100 — Fruity Pebbles
Per scoop120 cal · 25 g protein
Carbs2 g (1 g sugar)
Sodium100 mg
TimingPost-workout or any time you're short on protein
Why. Hydrolyzed whey isolate — fast absorbing, low fat/sugar, clean macro hit. One scoop closes the daily protein gap when meals fall short. Promix grass-fed whey is a clean alternative (~25 g protein per 2-scoop serving, ~135 cal).

Not on the list

Pre-workouts, BCAAs, fat burners, test boosters: skip them. They're either redundant when you're eating enough protein, or have weak evidence. Coffee is a fine pre-workout for free.

1,100kcal

The MVP meal. Huge protein, omega-3s from salmon (heart bonus), slow carbs from brown rice. Almost perfect.

Sodium catch. Teriyaki sauce is the salt source. Ask for half-sauce on the side — same flavor, drops sodium ~600-800 mg. Big win for the heart.

Sample 3,300-calorie day

Protein smoothie (600) + Glaze teriyaki (1,100) + Salmon & rice (700) + 1 protein shake + handful of almonds + apple (~300) = ~2,700 cal, 190 g protein, ~2,250 mg sodium.

You're under by 500-600 cal. Easy fix: 2 cups milk through the day (300 cal), 1 extra tbsp peanut butter (90 cal), or upsize the rice at lunch.
